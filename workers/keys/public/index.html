RIDE RECORDING

Your rides.
Your keys.

Make the two files that protect your saved rides. Your phone or computer makes them here; neither file is sent anywhere.

Keep the private one safe

The private key opens your recorded rides. Keep a backup in your password manager or another safe place, away from the Pi. If you lose it, your rides cannot be recovered.

Already have keys? Keep using them. A new pair cannot open rides saved with an older one. This is separate from your Bluetooth access code.
