# Did the fan loop ever stop applying its own answer?

Issue [#282](https://github.com/danieltroger/cool-eva/issues/282). Every figure in `docs/fan-control.md` §9 and `docs/ride-log-clock.md` §6 is generated by `curve-lag.py`, not retyped, and its committed output is `figures.txt`.

```sh
python3 evidence/fan-tick-stall/curve-lag.py > evidence/fan-tick-stall/figures.txt   # ~35 s
```

It reads `rides.db` **read-only** (`RIDES_DB=` overrides the path) and needs nothing else — no captures, no network, no Pi.

## What it measures

For every boot, what the fan loop decided against what it had already commanded, on both branches that set a duty: the pack-temperature curve, and a DC session. A divergence is opened when the two disagree and closed when they agree again; what is reported is how long one was ever **held**.

## Three things it must keep doing

- **Group by `session_id`, order by `seq`.** #282 exists because four boots' rows were read off one `ts` axis. `docs/ride-log-clock.md` §6.
- **Judge the DC branch too.** A detector that only judges `PACK_TEMPERATURE` is silent on the regime #282 was actually about, and the silence is self-concealing: a loop that dies while the reason is `DC_SESSION` leaves that reason latched under log-on-change, so the sample never becomes judgeable.
- **Find the clock steps over the whole table, at contiguous `seq`.** Deriving them from the script's own sparse row stream reads every quiet sampling gap as a step and refuses exactly the intervals worth measuring. That bug shipped once: it returned **zero** mutation kills at a 30 s grace while looking healthy at 5 s.

## The mutant

`BIAS_C` is a magnitude in degrees, not a flag. The committed column is `BIAS_C=7`; the real measurement is `BIAS_C=0`, which `main()` takes for the baseline column itself. A detector that cannot fire proves nothing, so the two columns are printed side by side rather than the baseline alone:

| | curve arm | DC arm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| real archive, 30 s grace | 0 held | 0 held |
| mutant +7, 30 s grace | 35 held, 13 refused | 49 held, 9 refused |

35 + 13 = 48, which is what the same sweep with no step guard at all reports — so the guard is subtracting steps and nothing else.
