# 0x412 — the instrument cluster's range estimate

`src/can/cluster-range.ts`. 2 Hz, present whenever the cluster is awake.

| b0    | b1                   | b2-b3                     | b4     | b5     | b6           | b7     |
| ----- | -------------------- | ------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------------ | ------ |
| state | varies at frame rate | **u16 LE — range, km** ✅ | `0x31` | `0x42` | unidentified | `0x00` |

Only **b2-b3** is decoded, as `range_can_km`.

## Why b2-b3 is the range

**The firmware computes it.** The packer at `0x00052C18`-`0x00052CC0` fills b2 from the low byte and b3 from the high byte of the u16 at `r13-0x7480` (`rlwinm r0, r3, 0x18, 0x18, 0x1f` takes the high half), so it is little-endian on the wire. That variable has one writer, `0x00064590`, which copies `r13-0x7a6e`. **`r13-0x7a6e` in turn has exactly one writer**, the block at `0x000644FC`-`0x00064530`:

```
000644FC  lis/addi r3, 0x6000B6F0
00064504  lwz   r3, 0xd4(r3)          ; [0x6000B7C4]
00064508  lhz   r0, -0x74f2(r13)
0006450C  mullw r0, r3, r0
00064510  lis/addi r3, 0x4F8B588F     ; divide-by-constant
00064518  mulhwu ; subf ; srwi 1 ; add ; srwi 0x10
00064530  sth   r0, -0x7a6e(r13)
```

The magic was solved numerically rather than recognised by eye: **the divisor is exactly 100 000**, checked against every multiple and every multiple-minus-one up to 400. So the field is a _computed estimate_ from two of the cluster's own variables — which is what a range estimate is, and what a relayed byte is not.

⚠️ **The value agreeing with `range_km` is NOT independent corroboration, and an earlier draft of this document claimed it was.** The cluster writes `r13-0x7a6e` into the `0x410` type-2 range slot as well (`0x000623B4`/`0x000623C0`), so `0x412` b2-b3 and the hub's `range_km` are **the same variable in two frames**. "Both read 79 in the same capture" was true by construction and could not have come out otherwise. It is the assertion-that-cannot-fail bug one level up.

**What the value match does establish** is the _meaning_: `range_km`'s semantics come from the Android app's `CommParser` via `src/ble/protocol.ts`, independently of anything here, and `0x412` carries that same number — 79 on 2026-08-02, 133 on 2026-08-19. That is an identification, not a second witness.

**The assumption underneath it was tested and held.** All of this rests on the cluster filling the hub's type-2 slots faithfully rather than approximately. The frame `02 00 2B 3C 3E 4F 00 1D` (2026-08-02) also carries SOC in b2 and pack temperature in b7 — and both of those reach the ride log from **CAN `0x200`, the BMS**, because `src/ble/protocol.ts` explicitly skips them on the Bluetooth path. `b2` = `0x2B` = 43 against `soc` **43.0**; `b7` = `0x1D` = 29 against `batt_temp_hi` **29.0**. Two slots, a different ECU, both exact. That could have failed and did not.

## What is not decoded, and why

**b6 — unidentified.** One writer, `0x00065EF0`: `[0x6000B6F0 + 0x1bc]` divided by 100, truncated to a byte. Values 4-17.

❌ **"b6 is the consumption the range estimate divides by" is REFUTED.** The range's only divisor is the compile-time 100 000 above, and b6's source is a _different_ struct field (`+0x1bc` against the range's `+0xd4`). The three-point story that suggested it — (43 % SOC, 79 km, b6 8), (50 % SOC, 64 km, b6 10), b2b3 × b6 landing at 632 and 640 — is a coincidence of two quantities that both track energy.

It is also **not** the hub's `kwh_per_100km`: an integer-truncated `/100` compared for exact equality against a two-decimal value could never have matched, so the 1/93 "match rate" an earlier draft quoted was an invalid test rather than a negative result.

⚠️ **The constant at `0x00065EDC` is `0x51EB851F`, not the `0x51EC851F` a reader takes from the `lis`** — `addi r3, r3, -0x7ae1` sign-extends. That is the same trap that once produced `0xC1A1B1BE` for the session key. `mulhwu(0x51EB851F, n) >> 5` is `n // 100` for every n to 200 000; the un-extended constant first diverges at n = 20 999.

**b0 and b1 are not constants.** An earlier draft said both were always `0x00`, from a single 90 s parked capture. The 2026-08-09 riding archive has b0 ∈ {`0x00`, `0x44`} and **27 distinct values of b1** over 10 939 frames, and the 2026-09-15 parked-on-AC capture is `03 06/08 40 00 31 42 0A 00` — b0 = `0x03`, b1 alternating at 2 Hz.

**b4, b5, b7.** `0x31 0x42 … 0x00` in all 10 939 frames of the riding capture and all 180 of 2026-08-02. One contrary measurement exists — 19 frames of b4 = `0x21` — which was not reproduced here, so these are "constant in everything measured", not constants.

## Rate and presence

2 Hz. Present parked, charging and riding — it and `0x400` are the only two of the cluster's transmit ids live on a parked bike (`evidence/cluster-ids-20260915T153020Z.log`, 25 s, 50 frames).
