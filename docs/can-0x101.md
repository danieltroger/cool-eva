# `0x101` `VCU_VEHICLE_STS` — the VCU's own state machine

Decoded in `src/can/vehicle-status.ts`, checked by `scripts/check-vehicle-status.ts`, 100 Hz.

Energica's frame database names all eight signals (the 2024 service-tool analysis in `obd-garage/`, §`0x101` `VCU_VEHICLE_STS`). Until 2026-09-14 the id was not in `STREAM_IDS`, so the kernel filter dropped every frame of it and nothing in this repo had ever seen one.

```
b0        V_VEHICLE_SUBSTATE          b3 &0x03  V_DRIVE_VSM (the duplicate)
b1        V_VEHICLE_STATE             b3 &0x04  V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS
b2        V_DRIVE_VSM                 b3 &0x08  V_LIMP_RES_VALID
b4-5 LE   V_LIMP_PACK_RES (short)     b6-7 LE   V_LIMP_MODULE_STS (short)
```

## The corpora

Two, a month apart, and every figure below says which it comes from.

- **The candump archive** — every candump-format log in `~/Documents/cool-eva-archive`: the 246 `capture-*.log` plus `ride-1.log`, `ride-2026-08-02.log`, `charge.log`, `charge-2026-08-02-real.log`, `buttons-2026-08-19.log` and the two `dtc-2026-08-02-*.log`. 253 files, 97 carrying 0x101, **15 006 844 frames**, 5 truncated lines excluded. At 100 Hz that is ≈41.7 h of frame-time; captures overlap in time, so it is frame-time and not unique wall-clock.
- **Three boots of 2026-09-13** — `evidence/captures/frames-0x101-*.txt`, **1 184 096 frames**: `150718` (3 h, including the 18:07 reverse stall), `180503` (the boot the bike fell over on) and `103237` (an ordinary ride). These carry the deliberate mode changes the archive never had.

⚠️ **The trap in the second corpus, because a wrong number nearly shipped off it.** Those files contain capture gaps and backward clock steps — `150718` one gap of 1 077 s and one backward step, `180503` three gaps and four backward steps, `103237` one gap of 4 429 s. A dwell computed as last-minus-first timestamp of a run silently spans them: a first pass here reported **"substate 53 held for 317 s"**, and that 317 s is a 310.1 s hole in `180503` between 18:05:15 and 18:10:26 with nothing recorded in it. Every dwell below is computed over **continuous segments only** — consecutive frames with 0 ≤ Δt ≤ 1 s — with any run touching a segment edge discarded (17 discarded). The `lean-tilt-check` lane retracted a 4 673.8 s figure to the same trap a day earlier.

## 🔥 The state words were already logged, over the other transport

`src/ble/protocol.ts` reads `V_VEHICLE_STATE` and `V_VEHICLE_SUBSTATE` out of the Connectivity Hub's BLE vehicle-status message and has written them as **`vehicle_state`** and **`vehicle_substate`** since June. So the CAN keys carry `_can`, the way `odometer_can_km` does beside the hub's `odometer_km` — one key with two writers flaps between them in `liveState` and interleaves in the ride log, and keeping them apart is what lets a ride say whether the two agree.

It also hands the decode a second witness that cost nothing. From `rides.db` (35 168 921 readings) against the archive:

|  | values, most frequent first |
| --- | --- |
| BLE `vehicle_state`, 365 rows | 60 (206), 40 (117), 100 (24), 20 (13), **4 (3)**, 80 (1), **0 (1)** |
| CAN b1, 15 006 844 frames | 100, 40, 60, 80, 20, 1 |
| BLE `vehicle_substate`, 451 rows | 62, 43, 52, 53, 104, 101, 34, 41, 109, **0**, 144, 105, 33, 22, 83, 20 |
| CAN b0, 38 values | a superset of every BLE substate except 0 — **including 144**, one of the bit-7 values |

Two transports, two decoders written months apart, one vocabulary. The BLE-only `4` and `0` are 4 rows of 365 (1.1 %) and read as that path's partial-frame sentinels.

⚠️ **This refutes a comment in the file it came from.** `src/ble/protocol.ts` said _"The range estimate and the vehicle state machine are **not on CAN at all** — 0x201 only carries charge state, not this."_ The vehicle state machine is on CAN, at 100 Hz, and has been all along. Corrected 2026-09-14.

## What the bytes do

### b1 / b0 — state and substate

b1 takes **exactly six values in 15 006 844 frames**, and each owns a band of substates. The band table is in the section further down, which this file inherits.

✅ **b0 ≥ 128 latches the state**: 1 748 frames, b1 unchanged from the preceding frame in **1 748 of 1 748**. The three-boot corpus reproduces it at 36 of 36.

### ✅ The drive-enable chain is deterministic

Every departure from `60/62` for drive walks the same nine substates in the same order — **9 times out of 9 across the three boots, with no variant**:

```
60/62  →  46 → 150 → 41 → 42 → 47 → 143 → 47 → 51 → 59 → 43
        0.10  0.01  0.23  0.10  0.01  0.03  0.01  0.01  0.01    seconds
                   -0.96                                        (41 is the only step that varies)
```

One of the nine chains is committed verbatim at **`evidence/x101/enable-chain-2026-09-13.txt`** (13 KB), and the one-frame `60/63` with its neighbours at **`evidence/x101/parked-entry-2026-09-13.txt`** — so both claims above are reproducible from this repo rather than from the 70 MB extract they were measured on, which is gitignored.

Two of the nine — **150 and 143** — have bit 7 set, which is where the archive's 1 748 bit-7 frames come from and what the latch rule above is describing. They are steps of a start-up sequence, not states.

✅ **The exit is one frame.** `60/63` occurs **9 times, every one exactly one frame (0.01 s)**, always immediately before `60/62`. It is the entry edge into parked.

### b2 `V_DRIVE_VSM` — the field Energica's own parser loses, and what it actually says

§A.3 of the vendor analysis lists this frame among the bugs in Energica's tool: `V_DRIVE_VSM = data[2];` then `V_DRIVE_VSM = (byte)(data[3] & 3);` — _"assigned twice; byte 2 lost"_. We decode the two as separate quantities, and byte 2 turns out to mean something.

❌ **A first reading of it was published in PR #234 and was over-claimed. It is retracted here rather than quietly rewritten**, because how it went wrong is the useful part. It said b2 was a _transition marker_ — _"reads 6 while state 40 is settled and drops to 4 for ~0.1 s as the substate changes"_ — on the strength of 107 of 107 substate changes carrying a `b2 == 4` frame. **The diff reviewer decoded this PR's own committed excerpt and found b2 is 4 for the ENTIRE 1.26 s enable chain**, and was already 4 for the parked frames before it. There is no dip there to see.

✅ **What holds is simpler.** b2 reads **6 in the three driving substates — 43 (riding), 52 (park assist) and 53 — and 4 everywhere else**, in every other substate of state 40 and in every other state:

| state/substate                                         | `b2 == 4`   | `b2 == 6`   |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | ----------- |
| 40/43 (riding)                                         | 317         | **706 631** |
| 40/52 (park assist)                                    | 87          | **26 752**  |
| 40/53                                                  | 75          | **2 712**   |
| 40/41, 42, 46, 47, 51, 59, 143, 150 (the enable chain) | **998**     | 0           |
| 60/62 (parked)                                         | **446 512** | 3           |
| 60/63 (the one-frame entry into parked)                | **8**       | 1           |
| **total**                                              | **447 997** | **736 099** |

The rows sum to **1 184 096**, which is every frame of the three boots — so this table is balanced rather than assembled, and nothing sits quietly outside it.

Two percentages, because the rule has two sides and they are not the same number:

- **Within the three driving substates, b2 reads 6 in 736 095 of 736 574 — 99.93 %.** The 479 exceptions are the dip below.
- **As the two-sided rule "6 if and only if a driving substate", 1 183 613 of 1 184 096 — 99.96 %.**

Archive-wide, b2 = 6 occurs only in state 40 — 3 949 871 of that state's 3 955 213 frames — the same rule seen from the other side, since 43/52/53 are where a bike in state 40 spends its time.

🟡 **The dip is real but much smaller than claimed.** Inside those three substates b2 _does_ drop to 4 for ~0.1 s at a substate change: **26 of 26** changes among 43/52/53 carry a `b2 == 4` frame within 15 frames, and the dips account for the 479 exception frames in the table. ⚠️ A lagged copy of b0 does not explain it — `43 → 52` is a change between two substates that both read 6, so a lagged copy would stay at 6 throughout.

⚠️ **Why the original 107/107 was not evidence for a marker.** Of the 107 substate changes inside state 40, **81 are in the enable chain**, where b2 is 4 for the whole second regardless — so those hits were free, and the statistic could not distinguish "marker" from "6 = in a driving substate". The 26 that remain are the ones that carry the claim, and 26 is the sample the 🟡 above rests on.

⚠️ **And the three-boot corpus carries four frames of b2 = 6 in state 60**, which the archive does not: three in `60/62` and one in `60/63`, the one-frame entry into parked. Four frames against 447 997 is a transition edge rather than a counterexample, but it is recorded because it is the only place the "6 only in state 40" rule bends — and because the first version of this sentence said _three_, off a table that had no `60/63` row in it. That is the same error as the one this section retracts, made in the sentence written to record it.

### 🟡 Substates 52 and 53 — a pair reachable only from riding

52 is park assist, identified from an earlier capture. The transition census over the three boots, gap-safe:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| entered `40/52` from | `40/43` 11×, `40/53` 2×, nothing else |
| entered `40/53` from | `40/52` **5×** — ⚠️ and two further episodes begin after a capture gap or a backward step, so their entry is unobservable. 5 of 7 observed, 2 censored. |
| left `40/52` for | `40/43` 5×, `40/53` 5×, `60/63` (park) 2× |
| left `40/53` for | `40/43` 3×, `40/52` 2×, `60/63` 1× |
| dwell | 52: 12 episodes, 5.75–89.78 s · 53: 5 episodes, 1.98–7.07 s |

Every observed entry into 53 is through 52, and the two alternate with 43 either side. That is the shape a two-direction park assist would have — a shape, not a measurement. Settling it wants `V_SPD_DIR` (0x104 bit 63) beside 0x101 in one file, which these extracts do not carry and #214 owns.

**The 18:07 reverse stall, as state:** key-on 18:06:59.28 → the nine-step chain → `40/43` 2.81 s → **`40/52` 89.78 s** → `60/63` → parked. The bike entered park assist and shut down out of it without returning to riding. Longest 52 on record by 8.2 s.

### b3 — two named bits, two unnamed ones that move, and one that never does

| bit   | name                              | archive                                                               |
| ----- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-1   | `V_DRIVE_VSM` (the duplicate)     | non-zero in 47 123 frames, all in state 40                            |
| 2     | `V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS`              | 🟡 **set in all 15 006 844** frames, and all 1 184 096 September ones |
| 3     | `V_LIMP_RES_VALID`                | set in 28 134                                                         |
| **4** | **not named by the vendor table** | set in 9 345 176 (62.3 %), only in states 100, 80, 20 and 1           |
| 5     | not named                         | never set                                                             |
| **6** | **not named by the vendor table** | set in 135 811, **only in state 40**                                  |
| 7     | not named                         | never set                                                             |

⚠️ **`V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS` is not the limp-mode flag it sounds like.** It is a constant 1 on this bike. It is decoded because a flag that only matters when it moves is worth having before it moves — `src/can/vcu-flags.ts`'s argument for eleven never-fired VCU error bits — and **nothing may gate on it**: "limp mode is active" and "the bit is stuck" are indistinguishable from every frame on record.

❌ **A claim this file used to make does not survive.** The §"b4 is not SOC" paragraph below says _"The b3 `4 → 0` on the fresh boot is `V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS` clearing — a flag, not a number."_ b3 takes ten values archive-wide — 20, 4, 68, 7, 12, 5, 71, 6, 15, 13 — and **every one of them has bit 2 set**; b3 = 0 occurs in none of the 15 006 844 frames. Not reproducible from the corpus on disk. (Scoped that way rather than called impossible: nothing here predates 2026-08-02.)

**Bits 4 and 6 are why the raw byte is logged** as `vehicle_status_flags`, beside the broken-out bits — the arrangement `vcu_flags_low`/`vcu_flags_high` already uses on 0x100, for the stated reason that a wrong bit position then costs a re-read of the log rather than a lost event. Naming a key for either would mean inventing a name the manufacturer does not give.

### b4-5 / b6-7 — the limp numbers

`V_LIMP_PACK_RES` takes 53 distinct values from 75 to 154 across the archive, and b5 is 0 in every frame. 🟡 `V_LIMP_MODULE_STS` is **0 in all 15 006 844 archive frames and all 1 184 096 September ones**.

Both are read **unsigned**, against the database's `short`. ⚠️ The two readings are indistinguishable on this corpus — the high bytes are always 0 — so the choice is made on shape rather than evidence: one is a resistance and the other a status word, and an unsigned read cannot turn a wrong-endian value into a plausible negative. ⚠️ **Neither carries a unit, and `limp_pack_res` must not be given one**: §A.4 of the vendor analysis states there are no scaling factors anywhere in it, so 75…154 being plausible for this pack's milliohms (`docs/pack-resistance.md`) is a resemblance and not a measurement. `db.ts` writes `signal` with `ON CONFLICT(key) DO NOTHING`, which freezes a unit for the life of the ride log.

## What the frame says when the boot is ending

Measured over the **80 last-of-boot captures** in the archive — a capture name carries the boot id, so every file of a boot but the last ended because `candump` restarted while the Pi was alive. `evidence/keyoff/`, and `docs/power-cuts.md` §7 for what the service does with it.

✅ **Entering state 60 precedes the end of the boot by 10.03–442.11 s**, median 64.39 s, over **16 observed entries** — every one of them at the one-frame `60/63` edge, which is what makes the entry observable at all. Not one is under 10 s. A further 12 terminal captures _open_ with the bike already in `60/62`; their file lengths (30.98–393.36 s) corroborate the bound but are not measurements of a park, and counting the two together once produced a wrong headline of 28 entries at a median of 93.03 s.

🟡 **Two shutdown vocabularies, both ending in silence within ~1.5 s.** Five terminal captures enter state 20 or state 1 within their last minute:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| `capture-20260802-203750-7ce067a7.log` | `100/102 → 100/109 → 100/113 → 1/3 → 1/6 → 1/9 → 1/2`, last frame **0.000 s** after `1/2` |
| `capture-20260809-211759-1956320f.log` | `100/109 → 100/113 → 1/3 → 1/6 → 20/20 → 20/28 → 20/22`, last frame **0.184 s** after `20/22` |
| `capture-20260802-185513-563dd217.log` | `100/110 → 100/112 → 20/23 → 20/26 → 20/31 → 20/32 → 20/33`, last frame **1.367 s** after `20/33` |

⚠️ The other two enter state 20 and then reach `60/62`, so **entering state 20 is not itself terminal** and nothing here may treat it as a shutdown marker.

❌ **Entering state 80 is not usable as one either.** 13 observed entries across the terminal captures, lead **0.38–1 847.70 s** with eleven under 11 s. The minimum is too short to act on and the spread argues harder against the signal than a tight band would.

**Coverage, because half of it is absence.** The last state of the 80 terminal captures is 60 in 35, 100 in 20, 80 in 14, 40 in 5, 20 in 5 and 1 in 1, and **38 of 80 never show state 60 at all** — a boot that ends while DC charging or in a blocking fault never announces anything.

⚠️ **The corpus is narrow**: 78 of the 80 fall in 2026-08-02…08-10, 22 of them on one day.

Measured by replaying the log-on-change rule `src/can/signals.ts` applies over the 97 archive files **in file sequence**, which is the only order that means anything for a log-on-change rule:

| key                    | rows  | key                | rows      |
| ---------------------- | ----- | ------------------ | --------- |
| `vehicle_substate_can` | 803   | `limp_mode_status` | 97        |
| `vehicle_state_can`    | 214   | `limp_res_valid`   | 320       |

| `vehicle_status_flags` | 1 086 | **total**          | **3 566** |

**≈86 rows/h of bike-on time**, against the 33 700 rows/h the 2026-08-16 frame batch measured for its 31 signals. The two constants cost 97 rows each, which is one per file — `signals.ts` logs the first sample of every signal after each boot, and that is what a constant looks like.

⚠️ **0x101 is a fifth 100 Hz id in `STREAM_IDS`** (with 0x102, 0x104, 0x109, 0x400), so ~100 more RX wakeups a second on the Pi, and **~900 more `record()` calls a second** — nine keys at 100 Hz, each a `Map` set, a `monotonicNow()` and a deadband compare. `src/can/decode.ts` counts the same cost for 0x400 and also records why the obvious mitigation is a trap: skipping payload-identical frames stops `record()` refreshing staleness, and a signal nobody is changing starts looking dead.

## What is still open

- **The meanings of the six states and most of the substates.** The enable chain, 83 (blocking fault), 62 (parked), 43 (riding) and 52 (park assist) are identified; the rest are numbers.
- **What separates 52 from 53.** Needs `V_SPD_DIR` in the same file.
- **What bits 4 and 6 of b3 are.** Both move, both track the state, neither has a name.
- ⚠️ **The two 16-bit fields' bounds are DECORATIVE, and are kept that way deliberately.** `public/lib/bounds.js` gates both to the whole 16-bit field, so `isPlausible()` accepts `0xFFFF` — the "no data" sentinel that file's own header cites for the cell voltages. A tighter ceiling needs a scale, and refusing to assert a scale is exactly why these two ship unitless; a number picked without one would be that assertion wearing a gate's clothes. So the gate catches a wrong field width and nothing else. Named here rather than left for rediscovery, the way `attitude_roll_deg`'s decorative band is named in `bounds.js`.
- **`V_LIMP_PACK_RES`'s scale and unit**, and whether it agrees with the pack resistance this repo derives (`docs/pack-resistance.md`) — a second opinion from the bike on a quantity we went to some trouble to compute.
- **Whether `V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS` ever clears.** Nothing on disk shows it.

## Method notes, so the next measurement does not inherit these

The sweeps behind this document are one-off scripts, not repo code. Four bugs in them, none of which changes a figure above: the `mag_good` clear-run histogram never flushes each file's final run (a small undercount); the indicator-switch attribution used a same-frame test rather than the 3 s window the archive measurement used, and is unused here; a frame with DLC < 8 is counted malformed where the real decoder decodes it partially (2 frames); and a later pass's timestamp helper discards the date, so it wraps at midnight and the archive has overnight captures (no press/onset pair straddles one). ## How this frame was got wrong first, kept in full

### ⚠️ `0x101` is `VCU_VEHICLE_STS`, and the name was in this repo the whole time

**Everything under this heading replaces a decode that was merged into `main` on 2026-09-08 and was wrong.** Two independent reviews plus an archive sweep of **15 006 844 `0x101` frames** — every capture in `~/Documents/cool-eva-archive` — took it apart. The correction is recorded in full rather than quietly rewritten, because how it went wrong is more useful than the answer.

**It was never a decode problem.** The 2024 service-tool analysis in `obd-garage/`, §`0x101` `VCU_VEHICLE_STS`, names the frame and all eight of its signals:

```
0x101  VCU_VEHICLE_STS
  b0        V_VEHICLE_SUBSTATE
  b1        V_VEHICLE_STATE
  b2        V_DRIVE_VSM
  b3 &0x03  V_DRIVE_VSM       &0x04 V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS   &0x08 V_LIMP_RES_VALID
  b4-5 LE   V_LIMP_PACK_RES   (short)
  b6-7 LE   V_LIMP_MODULE_STS (short)
```

That file is in this repository. It was not consulted, and days of statistics went into re-deriving a worse version of a table already on disk. ⚠️ **Before analysing an unknown frame, grep `obd-garage/` for its id.** The inventory of "unmapped frames" is a list of frames nobody looked up, not a list of frames without names.

#### ❌ The merged formula is false

```
b1 == floor(b0 / 20) * 20     claimed: 344 957 of 344 957 frames, zero exceptions
                              actual:  1 992 exception frames archive-wide
```

Seven b0 values break it — `2, 3, 6, 9` (b1 = 1, predicted 0) and `143, 144, 150` (b1 = 40, 100, 40, predicted 140). It was measured over six captures that happened to contain none of them. ⚠️ **A counterexample was named two paragraphs above the claim**: the inventory line quoted b0 spanning `29-96`, and `0x96` is 150.

A first correction offered in review — _"it holds wherever b0 < 128"_ — **is also false**: b0 = 2, 3, 6, 9 are all below 128 and all break it. Recorded because the near-miss fix is the tempting one.

#### ✅ What actually holds

b1 is a **state** and b0 its **substate**, so the arithmetic was a numbering convention misread as a computation. b1 takes exactly **six values in 15 million frames — `1, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100`** — and each owns a band of substates:

| b1 (`V_VEHICLE_STATE`) | b0 seen in that band (`V_VEHICLE_SUBSTATE`)      |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1                      | 2, 3, 6, 9                                       |
| 20                     | 20, 22, 23, 26, 28, 31, 32, 33, 34               |
| 40                     | 41, 42, 43, 46, 47, 51, 52, 53, 59               |
| 60                     | 62, 63                                           |
| 80                     | 83                                               |
| 100                    | 101, 102, 104, 105, 106, 107, 109, 110, 112, 113 |

`floor(b0/20)*20` fits the middle of that table by coincidence of numbering and fails at both ends — at the bottom because state 1 is not state 0, at the top because **b1 is capped at 100 and b0 is not**.

✅ **The out-of-band case has a clean rule of its own.** Whenever b0 has bit 7 set (143, 144, 150), b1 **holds its previous value** instead of following:

```
b0 >= 128:  b1 unchanged from the preceding frame     1 748 / 1 748 frames, zero exceptions
```

143 → 40 and 150 → 40 in a capture sitting in state 40; 144 → 100 in one sitting in state 100. A substate with bit 7 set does not belong to a state band at all — the state latches while it is present.

#### ✅ Substate 83 is the blocking fault

Aligned against `0x100` byte 3 bit 7 (`vcu_err_system_blocking_fault`) across the whole archive, last-seen alignment:

```
b0 = 83 while the blocking fault is set     194 947 frames
b0 = 83 while it is clear                        40 frames  (0.02%)
any other b0 while the fault is set               1 frame
```

**194 947 of 194 948 fault frames are substate 83**, and state 80 exists for essentially nothing else in 15 million frames. ⚠️ This section previously called that "three observations of it is not a decode" — a statement about the sample that had been looked at, which is the same error as the formula above, made twice in one section.

#### ❌ Two of my own refutations did not survive

- ❌ _"b0 is not SOC — `b0 == soc` in 0 of 230 620 samples, ranges disjoint."_ **Scoped far too widely from one capture.** In `capture-20260808-211445` the two are equal in 32 341 of 230 020 samples, r = +0.493. b0 is not SOC — it is a named substate — but the numbers offered as proof were an artefact of the capture chosen. The vendor name is the evidence; the statistics never were.
- ⚠️ _"b0 correlates −0.57 with speed and rpm."_ The warning attached to it was right and is kept, but the figure is capture-specific: archive-wide it ranges **−0.387 to −0.87**. It is now explained rather than merely distrusted — substates encode drive states, and drive states co-occur with speed. A correlation between a state enumeration and a physical quantity measures the schedule of the states, not a relationship between them.

#### ❌ b4 is not SOC either — it is `V_LIMP_PACK_RES`

Checked because b4 moved 100 → 75 between two captures and looked like a percentage. It is not: across eight captures b4 sits **dead constant while SOC sweeps** — 75 through SOC 25→60 (1 775 661 frames), 85 through 63→89, 146 through 29→39 — and it exceeds 100, which SOC cannot. ⚠️ **And the "two exact-equality hits" figure that first appeared here was itself a subset claim** — the same error this section is about. Archive-wide `b4 == soc` in **231 691 of 15 006 589 aligned frames**, 155 391 of them in one capture (`capture-20260809-080235-cd40b535`) where b4 sweeps 75-129. Coincidental equality is _common_ for a byte that lives near the SOC range, which is exactly why equality counts are weak evidence in either direction and the constancy-while-SOC-sweeps test is the one that settles it. b4 takes 53 distinct values from 75 to 154 across the archive. 🟡 b5-7 are 0 in all 2 105 072 frames of the widest capture, so the `b4-5` short equals b4, and its 75-146 range sits in the plausible band for pack resistance (`docs/pack-resistance.md`). ❌ ~~The b3 `4 → 0` on the fresh boot is `V_LIMP_MODE_STATUS` clearing — a flag, not a number.~~ **Refuted 2026-09-14**: bit 2 is set in all 15 006 844 archive frames and b3 = 0 occurs nowhere in them. See §"b3" above.

#### What was left open as of 2026-09-08 (answered above where it has been)

The **meanings** of the six states and their substates. The names give the structure, not the vocabulary: nothing yet says which state is "ready", which is "charging", or what separates substate 43 from 62. That wants a capture with deliberate, logged mode changes — key-on, drive, reverse, charge, fault — not more correlation against the archive.
